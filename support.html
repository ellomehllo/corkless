Support

tack is a campus noticeboard for small paid jobs. Students pin a job, classmates bid, the poster picks one person, and the two of them sort it out in chat. Payment happens directly between members, in cash or UPI. tack never handles money.

Get help

In the app, open Me → menu → Report or feedback. You can report a problem with a job or a person, send feedback, or ask about your account and data. Only the organiser sees what you send. We acknowledge every report within 24 hours and aim to resolve it within 15 days.

You can also email us at .

Report or block someone

On any job, chat or profile, tap the three dots and choose Report or Restrict or block. The person isn't told who reported them. Posts with hate, abuse or illegal content are blocked before they go up.

Delete your account

Open Me → menu → Settings and privacy → Delete my account and confirm with your password. Your profile, jobs, bids, ratings you gave and messages are erased, and your login is removed.

Who can join

tack is for students who are 18 or older. You join with your college email, or with an invite from the organiser or a friend.
